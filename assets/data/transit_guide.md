## Voiceless Consonants

There are 5 Japanese vowels:

- a [ah] あ
- i [ee] い
- u [oo] う
- e [eh] え
- o [oh] お

Typically, "i" and "u" are reduced or eliminated when they are sandwhiched between voiceless consonants or at the end of a sentence (vowel divorcing). Voiceless consonants include (rows):

- K
  - ka [kah] か
  - ki [kee] き
  - ku [kuu] く
  - ke [keh] け
  - ko [koh] こ
- S
  - sa [sah] さ
  - si [see] し
  - su [suu] す
  - se [seh] せ
  - so [soh] そ
- T
  - ta [tah] た
  - ti [tee] ち
  - tu [tuu] つ
  - te [teh] て
  - to [toh] と
- H
  - ha [hah] は
  - hi [hee] ひ
  - hu [huu] ふ
  - he [heh] へ
  - ho [hoh] ほ
- P
  - pa [pah] ぱ
  - pi [pee] ぴ
  - pu [puu] ぷ
  - pe [peh] ぺ
  - po [poh] ぽ

Examples:

1. liking [suki] すき
   - Sounds like "ski" rather than "su-kee"
2. down [shita] した
   - Sounds like "shta" rather than "shi-ta"
3. I'm David [David desu] David です
   - Sounds like "David dess" rather than "David de-su"
4. to eat [tabemasu] たべます
   - Sounds like "tah-beh-mass" rather than "tah-beh-mah-suu"

## Kudasai vs Onegai shimasu

Kudasai is a command form of kudasaru, which is an honorific word (kureru). It's like saying "I very humbly COMMAND you to give me water." Onegai shimasu comes from "negau," which means to wish for or beg, along with the honorific.

- Use onegai shimasu with nouns
  - okaike onegai shimasu > check, please
  - Tokyo eki onegai shimasu > Tokyo Station, please
- Use kudasai with verbs
  - yukkuri hanashite kudasai > please speak slowly
  - moichido itte kudasai > please say it one more time

## Color Block Conversion

General Layout:

- Who (orange)
- Time (teal)
- Place (green)
- What (red)
- How (purple)
- Verb (blue)

- English
  - I drank coffee at a cafe yesterday.
- English (colors)
  - I (orange) drank (blue) coffee (red) at a cafe (green) yesterday (teal).
- Romanji
  - Watashi wa kinō kafede kōhīo nomimashita.
- Romanji (colors)
  - Watashi wa (orange) kinō (teal) kafede (green) kōhīo (red) nomimashita (blue).
- Kana
  - わたし わ きのう カフェで コーヒーを のみました.
- Kana (colors)
  - わたし わ (orange) きのう (teal) カフェで (green) コーヒーを (red) のみました (teal).

- English
  - I watched a movie with my friend at home yesterday.
- English (colors)
  - I (orange) watched (blue) a movie (red) with my friend (purple) at home (green) yesterday (teal).
- Romanji
  - Watashi wa kinō iede eigao tomodachito mimashita.
- Romanji (colors)
  - Watashi wa (orange) kinō (teal) iede (green) eigao (red) tomodachito (purple) mimashita (blue).
- Kana
  - わたし わ きのう いえで えいがを ともだちと みました.
- Kana (colors)
  - わたし わ (orange) きのう (teal) いえで (green) えいがを (red) ともだちと (purple) みました (teal).

## How to Make Japanese Sentences

Japanese Sentence Rule: subject > object > verb

"Talk like a S.O.B. (son of a bitch)!"

- English: I eat sushi.
- Japanese: I sushi eat.
- Romaji: watashi wa sushi o tabemasu
- Kana: わたし わ すし お たべます

Verb comes last. Always.

Breakdown:

- Subject - who is doing the action
- Object - what receives the action
- Verb - the action (always last)
- I (subject) sushi (object) eat (verb)

Another:

- English: He drinks water.
- Japanese: He water drink.
- Romaji: kare wa mizu o nomimasu
- Kana: かれ わ みず お のみます

### The Two Imporant Particles

- wa (は) - marks the topic / subject
- o (を) - marks the object

- English: I eat sushi.
- Japanese: I sushi eat.
- Romaji: watashi WA sushi O tabemasu
- Kana: わたし は すし を たべます

- wa (は) - tells us who
- o (を) - tells us what

## Particles

- wa (は) - subject
  - I am Ram
  - watashi WA Ramu desu
  - 私はラムです
- ga (が) - subject
  - I like ramen
  - watashi wa raāmen GA suki desu
  - 私はラーメンが好きです
- ka (か) - question
  - who are you?
  - anata wa dare desu ka
  - あなたは誰ですか
- no (の) - belonging
  - my book
  - watashi no hon
  - わたし の ほん
- mo (も) - also
  - I'll also eat
  - わたし も たべます
- o (を) - object
  - I eat sushi
  - sushi o tabemasu
  - すしをたべます
- ni (に) - time / location
  - will meet at 7
- de (で) - place of action / means
  - eat by chopsticks
- e (へ) - towards / directions
  - go to park
- to (と) - inclusive
  - sushi and ramen
- made (まで) - until
  - until 7
- kara (から) - from
  - I am from USA

## Common Kanji

- 入 (iri / nyuu) > enter
- 入口 > entrance
- 出 (de / shutsu) > exit
- 出口 > exit
- 男 (otoko / dan) > man
- 男性 (dansei) > male
- 女 (onna / jo) > woman
- 女性 (josei) > female
- 駅 (eki) > station
- 円 (en) > yen
  - 千円 (sen-en) > ¥1,000 yen
- 大 (dai / ō) > big
- 小 (shō / ko) > small
- 食 (shoku / ta) > eat / food
- 飲 (in / nomu) > drink
- 禁煙 (kin'en) > no smoking
- 無料 (muryou) > free / free of charge
- 有料 (yuuryou) > paid
- 営業中 (eigyou chuu) > open
